~/lab

Lab

Light a camera cannot catch, I work out with code. Light it did catch, I take apart into letters. Each experiment starts from a question.

If gravity bends light, what does the picture look like?

Black hole renderer

A program that draws what the space around a black hole looks like. It works out the path of light rays from physics equations, so the glowing ring and the bent starlight come from calculation rather than from painting. The look is inspired by the film Interstellar.

About

I photograph light, work it out with code, and take it apart into letters.

I am Ezra, a backend and platform engineer. I help teams that already have a working product with the parts nobody sees: the database, the background jobs, and the way different parts of a system pass messages to each other.

Outside work I take pictures, render light from physics, and turn photographs into text. Every picture on this site appears as letters first.
